# Team Synergy - Physical Card Game Guide

## Overview
Team Synergy is a trick-based card game designed to teach Renault engineers about the benefits of diverse work teams through engaging gameplay.

---

## Components Needed

### Card Deck (52 cards total)
- **4 Suits** representing diversity dimensions:
  - ★ **Skills** (Green) - 13 cards
  - ◆ **Perspectives** (Blue) - 13 cards
  - ● **Experience** (Orange) - 13 cards
  - ■ **Background** (Purple) - 13 cards

- **Card Values**: 1-13 in each suit
- **Each card includes**:
  - Value (1-13)
  - Suit symbol and name
  - Role description

### Additional Materials
- 10 Challenge Cards
- Score sheets
- Pencils
- Diversity bonus tracker tokens (optional)

## Game Setup (3-4 Players)

1. **Shuffle** the 52-card deck thoroughly
2. **Select** 10 Challenge Cards and place face-down
3. **Deal** 10 cards to each player
4. **Reveal** the first Challenge Card
5. Players organize their hands by suit
6. Determine first player (youngest player, or draw highest card)

---

## How to Play

### Objective
Build diverse teams to solve engineering challenges. The player with the most points after 10 rounds wins!

### Round Structure

#### 1. Lead Phase
- Lead player plays any card from their hand
- This card's suit becomes the **lead suit** for this trick
- The card is placed face-up in the center

#### 2. Follow Phase
- Each other player (clockwise) plays one card
- **Must follow suit** if possible
- If you don't have the lead suit, you may play any card

#### 3. Evaluate Phase
- **Winner**: Highest card of the lead suit wins the trick
- **Calculate Points**:
  - Base Points = Sum of all card values
  - Diversity Bonus:
    - All 4 suits present = **+10 points**
    - 3 different suits = **+5 points**
    - 2 or fewer suits = No bonus
- Winner takes the cards and records their score

#### 4. Continue
- Winner of the trick leads the next round
- Continue until all 10 cards are played

### After 10 Rounds
- Count total scores
- Highest score wins!
- In case of tie, player with most tricks wins

## Scoring System

### Base Points
Each card is worth its face value (1-13 points)

### Diversity Bonuses
- **Full Diversity Bonus (+10 points)**: Team has all 4 suits
  - ★ Skills + ◆ Perspectives + ● Experience + ■ Background
- **Synergy Bonus (+5 points)**: Team has exactly 3 different suits
- **No Bonus (0 points)**: Team has 2 or fewer suits

### Example Scoring

**Example 1: Full Diversity**
- Skills ★ 7 (Software Engineer)
- Perspectives ◆ 5 (Detail Oriented)
- Experience ● 3 (Senior)
- Background ■ 9 (Multilingual)

Base: 7 + 5 + 3 + 9 = **24 points**
Bonus: All 4 suits = **+10 points**
**Total: 34 points**

**Example 2: Good Synergy**
- Skills ★ 13 (Product Manager)
- Skills ★ 8 (System Architect)
- Perspectives ◆ 12 (Tech Enthusiast)
- Experience ● 6 (Tech Lead)

Base: 13 + 8 + 12 + 6 = **39 points**
Bonus: 3 different suits = **+5 points**
**Total: 44 points**

**Example 3: Low Diversity**
- Skills ★ 11 (Mobile Developer)
- Skills ★ 9 (Frontend Developer)
- Skills ★ 4 (Quality Tester)
- Perspectives ◆ 7 (Risk Taker)

Base: 11 + 9 + 4 + 7 = **31 points**
Bonus: Only 2 suits = **0 points**
**Total: 31 points**

## Strategy Tips

### For Winning
1. **Lead with diversity in mind**: Try to force diverse card combinations
2. **Save high cards**: Keep valuable cards for crucial moments
3. **Track played suits**: Remember which suits have been played
4. **Balance points vs. diversity**: Sometimes a smaller diverse team beats a large homogeneous one

### Advanced Tactics
1. **Suit counting**: Track which suits opponents are short on
2. **Strategic dumping**: Lose low-value tricks to win high-value diverse ones
3. **Timing bonuses**: Plan to create 4-suit tricks in high-value rounds

## Facilitation Guide for Renault

### Learning Objectives
After playing, participants should understand:
1. Diverse teams combine different strengths
2. Complementary skills create synergy
3. Variety in perspectives leads to better outcomes
4. Inclusive teams outperform homogeneous ones

### Debrief Questions (15-20 minutes)

**During the Game:**
- "What did you notice about diverse vs. homogeneous teams?"
- "When did the diversity bonus make a difference?"
- "How did you decide which cards to play?"

**After the Game:**
- "How does this relate to real engineering projects?"
- "What types of diversity did the game represent?"
- "Can you think of a time when diversity helped solve a problem at Renault?"
- "What barriers prevent diverse team formation in real life?"
- "How can we actively build more diverse teams?"

**Key Discussion Points:**
1. **Skills diversity**: Different technical abilities complement each other
2. **Perspective diversity**: Various thinking styles reduce blind spots
3. **Experience diversity**: Mixing junior and senior creates mentorship
4. **Background diversity**: Different cultures/industries bring fresh approaches

### Real-World Applications
- Team formation for new projects
- Hiring strategies
- Meeting participation
- Problem-solving sessions
- Innovation workshops

## Game Variants

- Deal 13 cards each

- Remaining cards form a draw pile (not used)

### Speed Variant (30 minutes)
- Play only 5 rounds instead of 10
- Deal 5 cards each
- Faster gameplay for time constraints

### Tournament Mode
- Multiple tables of 4 players
- Winners advance to finals
- Track cumulative scores across games

### Team vs. Team
- 4 players in 2 teams of 2
- Partners sit opposite
- Teams combine scores

## FAQ

**Q: Can I play any card if I don't have the lead suit?**
A: Yes! If you can't follow suit, you may play any card from your hand.

**Q: What if two cards have the same value?**
A: Only cards of the lead suit can win. The first card played breaks ties (goes to lead player).

**Q: Can I win with a high card of a different suit?**
A: No. Only cards matching the lead suit can win the trick.

**Q: How long does a game take?**
A: Typically 30-45 minutes for experienced players, 45-60 minutes for first-time players.

**Q: Can we play with more than 4 players?**
A: The game is optimized for 3-4 players. With more players, deal fewer cards per person.

**Q: Is there a digital version?**
A: Yes! Visit the web version or install the Microsoft Teams app.
