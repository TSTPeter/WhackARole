Team Synergy - Printable Cards

Printing Instructions:

  1. Use 300gsm cardstock for best results
  2. Print in color
  3. Cut along the card borders
  4. Optional: Laminate or use card sleeves for durability
  5. You will need to print card backs separately (page at the end)

Total cards to print: 52 playing cards + 10 challenge cards + card backs
