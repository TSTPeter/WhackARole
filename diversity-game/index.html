Team Synergy

Experience the Power of Diverse Teams

A trick-based card game that demonstrates how diverse teams solve complex engineering challenges more effectively.

  • Different perspectives lead to innovative solutions
  • Varied experiences strengthen problem-solving
  • Complementary skills create synergy
  • Inclusive teams outperform homogeneous ones

How to Play

Objective

Build the most diverse and effective teams to solve engineering challenges. The player with the most points at the end wins!

Card Types

★ Skills - Technical expertise (Software, Hardware, Design, Testing)
◆ Perspectives - Thinking styles (Analytical, Creative, Strategic, Practical)
● Experience - Career stages (Junior, Mid-level, Senior, Expert)
■ Background - Cultural diversity (Different regions, languages, industries)

Gameplay

  1. Each player receives 10 cards
  2. A challenge card is revealed (e.g., "Autonomous Vehicle Safety System")
  3. Lead player plays a card from their hand
  4. Other players must follow suit or play any card
  5. The trick is won by the highest card of the lead suit
  6. Diversity Bonus: Teams with all 4 different suits get +10 points!
  7. Player with most points after 10 rounds wins

Scoring

Base Points: Card value (1-13)

Diversity Bonus: +10 points for having all 4 suit types

Synergy Bonus: +5 points for having 3 different suits
